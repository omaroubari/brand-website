## Logo

### Safezone

Clear space is a protective buffer. It keeps the logo legible and stops surrounding
elements from crowding it, whatever the layout.

<LogoClearspace brand={props.brand} ui={props.ui} mark="logotype" />

### Usage

Three approved combinations, and no others. The first is primary; reach for the rest only
when the background forces it.

<LogoColorways brand={props.brand} mark="logotype" cols={3} />

### Scaling

Proportions never change, whether the logo is on a billboard or a business card. Below the
minimum, the logotype stops reading — switch to the brandmark rather than shrinking
further.

<SpecList
  items={[
    { term: "Min digital", value: "96 px" },
    { term: "Min print", value: "20 mm" },
    { term: "Brandmark", value: "24 px / 8 mm" },
  ]}
/>

### Misusage

Six ways to break the logo. Each one is a real mistake we have had to correct.

<Callout ui={props.ui} tone="dont" title="Also never">

Add a drop shadow, outline, gradient or bevel. Place the logo on a busy photograph without
a solid field behind it. Redraw or trace any part of the artwork.

</Callout>
