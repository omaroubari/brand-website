<Block title="Icons" subtitle="Usage">
<Fragment slot="note">

Icons support words; they rarely replace them. An icon alone is only acceptable where the
meaning is universal — close, search, play — or where a label appears on hover or focus.

Scale in whole multiples of the grid: 16, 24, 32, 48. At 16 px the 1.5 px stroke needs to
be redrawn rather than scaled, or it turns to mush.

<Callout tone="dont">

Do not fill line icons, mix them with a filled set, recolour them to the accent for
emphasis, or rotate them to invent a new meaning. Ask for a new icon instead.

</Callout>

</Fragment>
