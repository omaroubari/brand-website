<Block title="Archetypes" subtitle="Creator & Jester">
<Fragment slot="note">

**Creator.** We exist to make things. The work is inventive, precise, and driven by a
belief that a better-made object communicates better. This is what clients buy.

**Jester.** We refuse to be solemn about it. Wit and a light touch keep the work
approachable and stop precision from tipping into coldness. This is what people remember.

Lead with the Creator when the stakes are high — contracts, proposals, crisis
communications. Let the Jester show in the places nobody expects it.

</Fragment>

<Statement>
	A creative force that produces exceptional design, and does it in a playful,

<Block title="Writing" subtitle="In practice">
<Fragment slot="note">

Short sentences. Lead with the thing that matters. Plain words where a technical one would
only prove we know it. Address the reader as *you*.

</Fragment>

<Grid cols={2}>

<Callout tone="do" title="Do">

Write short sentences. Lead with the thing that matters. Use plain words. Address the
reader as *you*.

</Callout>

<Callout tone="dont" title="Don't">

Reach for *synergy*, *leverage*, *bespoke solutions*, or three adjectives where one works.
Never use exclamation marks to manufacture enthusiasm the sentence has not earned.

</Callout>
