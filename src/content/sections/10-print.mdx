## Promotional materials

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="note">

Everyday objects that carry the mark: mug, cap, tote, T-shirt. Bold where the object
allows it, restrained where it does not.

<Callout tone="note" title="Small applications">

Embroidery, engraving and screen print all lose fine detail. Below 20 mm use the
brandmark, single colour, with no attempt to reproduce hairlines.

</Callout>

</Fragment>

## Signage & displays

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="note">

Signage is read at distance and at an angle. Increase clear space to twice the standard
and check contrast against the actual wall colour, not a rendering of it.

</Fragment>

## Production

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="note">

Always proof the accent on the actual stock. It shifts noticeably on uncoated paper, and
that shift is the single most common reason a print job comes back wrong.
