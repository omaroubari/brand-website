---
title: Contact and social profiles
description: Provide contact details and official social accounts.
---

Use `brand.contact` for official channels your guidelines can display. The
object is required, but `{}` is valid when no contact information is public.

Replace `brand.contact` in `brandtree.config.ts` with this partial config example:

```ts title="brandtree.config.ts — brand.contact"
contact: {
  email: "brand@example.com",
  website: "https://example.com",
  phone: "+44 20 7946 0000",
  address: ["123 Example Street", "London"],
  socials: [{
    id: "instagram",
    label: "Instagram",
    handle: "@acme",
    url: "https://instagram.com/acme",
  }],
},
```

Use a stable social `id` even if the account's displayed label changes.
[Localization](/localization) supports translated addresses and social labels;
account URLs and handles stay canonical.
