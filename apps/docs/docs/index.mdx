Brandtree turns your brand data and written guidelines into a static website.
It supplies navigation, light and dark themes, and components for showing logos,
colors, typography, and other parts of your identity.

**Define reusable brand facts once in `brandtree.config.ts`. Write your guidelines
in Markdown or MDX.** Components connect the two: change a configured color and
every exhibit that uses it updates.

Create a project directly by running the following command:

```sh
pnpm dlx brandtree init
```

[Quickstart](/quickstart) includes npm and Bun alternatives and walks through
your first page.

## How a project fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure the brand">

`brandtree.config.ts` holds shared facts such as the palette, logo artwork,
typography, and contact information. It also holds site settings.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the guidelines">

Pages in `content/` explain how to use your identity. MDX lets you add
components that display the configured brand data alongside that explanation.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and publish">

The CLI prepares an Astro runtime, validates your inputs, and builds a static
website into `dist/` for your hosting provider.

  </Step>
</Steps>
